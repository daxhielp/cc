# AI Engineering Intern - Summer 2027

Location: New York, NY
URL: https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/addepar1/jobs/8927543002

Who We Are

Addepar is a global data and AI platform empowering investment professionals to turn complex financial information into actionable intelligence. Addepar unifies portfolio, market and client data in a total portfolio view and delivers AI-powered insights within investment and client workflows. More than 1,500 firms in 60 countries use Addepar to manage and advise on nearly $10 trillion in assets. Its open platform integrates with 650 software, data and consulting partners to power end-to-end investment operations across firms of all sizes and levels of complexity. Addepar supports clients worldwide with offices in New York City, Salt Lake City, São Paulo, London, Edinburgh, Geneva, Warsaw, Dubai, Pune and Singapore.

The Program

We are currently seeking an AI Engineering Intern to join Addepar's Core AI team in New York City for Summer 2027.

Being a part of the AddeU Internship Program, you will be working on meaningful project(s) that you can feel proud of and present back to the company at the end of the experience. In addition to having a supporting manager and mentor, we have a fantastic onboarding program to get you acclimated and ready for your time with us. Fun events and super cool swag are also included!

AI is transforming how investment professionals work, and Addepar's Core AI team is at the center of that transformation. The team builds the full AI platform, supports applied AI teams with reusable components, and drives AI innovation and research across the company. If you are passionate about applying cutting-edge AI techniques to real-world financial technology problems, we want to hear from you!

As an AI Engineering Intern, you will work at the frontier of applied AI in fintech, contributing to the tools and platform that power intelligent features across Addepar's entire product suite.

This role is based full-time in our New York office and requires in-person attendance for the duration of the internship. Dates are currently set for June - September 2027 but please advise your recruiter if you have any timing constraints. Relocation assistance is not offered.

Applicants must be legally authorized to work in the United States for any employer without requiring current or future visa sponsorship (for example, employment-based visas such as H-1B, F-1/OPT, or similar), and must be authorized to begin work in the U.S. on their first day of employment.

Addepar takes a market-based approach to pay. A successful candidate’s starting pay will be determined based on the role, job-related skills, experience, qualifications, work location, and market conditions. The program rate for this internship is $54 per hour.

Compensation for this role includes hourly pay + benefits. This internship is not eligible for bonus or equity.

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis between November 2026 and March 2027. We encourage you to apply early, as we may close the posting once the program is filled.

What You'll Do

- Build and extend components of Addepar's core AI platform and tooling

- Prototype and experiment with emerging AI/ML techniques for fintech applications

- Collaborate with the Core AI team on projects with clear, measurable impact

- Participate in code reviews, model evaluations, and team ceremonies

- Document your work including model architecture, evaluation results, and design decisions

- Produce a retrospective and demonstrate your summer project to an internal audience
