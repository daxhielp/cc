# JMP C++ Software Development Intern

JMP 2027 Summer Internship; C++ Software Development

Requisition ID

20072219

Primary Location - Country

United States

Visa Sponsorship

No

Travel Requirements

Looking for *that* internship? The game-changing one that will help you learn, grow, and chart your path forward? You’ll find it at JMP (a subsidiary of SAS)! Our interns aren’t coffee runners – they do real, meaningful work. Our award-winning internship program is focused on development, culture, and community. We’ll help you grow professionally, find (or further) your passion, and make memorable connections that last beyond the summer.     

About the job:
The Interactive HTML team at JMP is responsible for bringing interactive graphical displays from JMP Statistical Discovery desktop software to the web as content within our JMP Live web application. We give our users the ability to share their creations and discoveries from our Mac and Windows versions of JMP by using C++ to extract relevant information and produce a high level of interactivity using JavaScript and HTML5 technologies.

As an intern, you might: 
- Extend the number of interactive graphics that JMP users can publish to the web
- Enhance existing web-based interactive graphs
- Learn and apply software engineering techniques to ensure quality
- Create examples that demonstrate the utility of the new functionality
Required Qualifications: 
- Targeted Degree/Majors: Bachelor's in Computer Science or Computer/Software Engineering
- You've got programing experience in C++ and a desire to learn JavaScript and HTML/CSS
- You’re curious, passionate, authentic, and accountable. These are our values and influence everything we do
- Strong communication skills – both written and verbal
- Leadership abilities. Your past experiences demonstrate you’ll take initiative and go above and beyond the call of duty
- You’re interested in the future of Analytics and embrace technology
- You’re a college student enrolled in an accredited program, not graduating prior to December 2027.
Preferred Qualifications 
- Experience with or strong understanding of software engineering practices such as design, development, testing, and technical documentation
- Ability to break down complex challenges into smaller manageable tasks
- Ability to follow detailed instructions and best programming practices 
Location 
- Our team is a mixture of developers working remotely and in a hybrid fashion (2 or more days per week in the office) 

Timeline  
- Applications open: November 2026 
- Interviews begin: November 2026 on a rolling basis.
- Internship: May 18, 2027 – August 6, 2027 (These dates are not flexible. To be considered for the JMP Summer Internship program, all interns must be available to start in person at Cary HQ on May 18, 2027. Summer internships last 12 weeks.)

Perks of the job    
- Work with (and learn from) the best. As a JMP intern, you’ll get face time with company leaders and executives.   
- Hybrid work flexibility: Our hybrid approach offers the best of both worlds, combining in-office collaboration with the flexibility to work remotely, aligned with your team’s needs.
- Free SAS programming training and certification.      
- Your well-being matters. We support all dimensions of your well-being with programs designed to reduce stress and distractions so you can stay healthy and productive. This includes an on-site Work/Life Center, Recreation and Fitness Center, Employee Assistance Program (EAP), subsidized cafés and more.    
- We work hard, but we like to have fun, too. Enjoy hackathons, social events and other opportunities to connect and engage.    

You are welcome here.
